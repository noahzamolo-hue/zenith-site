# SOP — ATP Bioluminescence Testing

**Goal:** an *objective, numeric* cleanliness score for a surface. ATP (adenosine triphosphate) is present in organic residue — biological soil, body fluids, food. A luminometer measures it in **RLU** (Relative Light Units). Lower RLU = cleaner surface. This is the single most defensible number you can put in front of an accreditation-conscious clinic.

## Kit (~$350–500)
- Handheld ATP luminometer (e.g. Hygiena SystemSURE / EnSURE, 3M Clean-Trace, or equivalent)
- Single-use ATP surface swabs (ongoing consumable, ~$3–4 each)
- Cool, dry storage for swabs

## Thresholds (confirm against YOUR device)
Manufacturers calibrate differently — set the numbers below in Job Form 3 to match your unit's documentation. A common general guide:

| Zone / surface | Pass (clean) | Caution | Fail (re-clean) |
|----------------|--------------|---------|-----------------|
| 🟡 Yellow / clinical | < 250 RLU | 250–500 | > 500 |
| 🔴🟢🔵 Red / Green / Blue | < 500 RLU | 500–1000 | > 1000 |

> Clinical surfaces are held to the stricter threshold because of infection risk. Always defer to your device manufacturer's benchmark if it differs.

## Procedure
1. **Test AFTER cleaning** — ATP measures the result of the clean, so swab a surface that's been cleaned and dried.
2. **Swab a defined area** — ~10cm × 10cm, firm S-pattern strokes, rotating the swab tip.
3. **Activate & read** — snap the swab per device instructions, insert into the luminometer, read the RLU (usually ~15 seconds).
4. **Record** — enter RLU and Pass/Fail per surface in **Job Form 3 → ATP swab readings**.
5. **Act on a Fail** — re-clean the surface, re-swab, confirm it now passes. Log both readings.
6. **Same points as UV** — swab the same high-touch points you UV-mark so the two methods corroborate.

## Which surfaces
5 swabs per site minimum, weighted to clinical:
- Yellow: exam bed surface, clinical door handle
- Red: bathroom tap
- Green: reception counter
- Blue: kitchen bench

## Frequency
- Monthly baseline; **weekly for Yellow zones** at higher-risk sites or new contracts (first 8 weeks) to establish a clean baseline.

## Target
- **≥ 90% of clinical swabs under threshold** (KPI #4). Trend the RLU values on the dashboard — a rising trend flags a slipping clean *before* the client notices.

## Client-facing value
Put the month's RLU readings (with pass thresholds) into the compliance PDF. "Independently ATP-tested, all clinical surfaces under 250 RLU" is a claim no pen-and-paper cleaner can make — and exactly what an accreditor likes to see.
